Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

This Refund Policy applies to any paid services or digital products offered by Jaoguya, including but not limited to freelance development work, consulting services, and digital deliverables. If you are visiting this website solely as a portfolio, no commercial transaction or refund obligation exists.

2. Freelance & Development Services

For custom development or consulting work:

  • Before project commencement: A full refund of any deposits paid may be requested if no work has begun
  • During active development: Partial refunds may be available, calculated based on work completed to date. Completed milestones are non-refundable
  • After delivery: Once the final deliverable has been approved and handed over, refunds are generally not available

3. Digital Products

If applicable, digital products (templates, tools, educational content) are subject to the following:

  • Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of purchase
  • The product must not have been substantially used or deployed
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 14 business days

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us with:

  • Your full name and email address used for the transaction
  • A description of the service or product
  • The reason for your refund request
  • Any relevant documentation (invoices, receipts, correspondence)

5. Exceptions

Refunds may not be available in the following cases:
