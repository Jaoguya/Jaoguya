Cookies Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience. Cookies may be "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent" cookies (remain until they expire or you delete them).

2. How We Use Cookies

This website uses a minimal number of cookies, categorized as follows:

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They are set without requiring your consent.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
jaoguya-theme Stores your dark/light theme preference Persistent (localStorage)
jaoguya-cookie-consent Records your cookie consent decision Persistent (localStorage)

2.2 Analytics Cookies (Optional)

These cookies are only loaded after you explicitly accept cookies via the cookie consent banner. They help us understand how visitors interact with the website by collecting anonymous usage statistics.

If you decline cookies, no analytics or tracking cookies will be set.

2.3 Third-Party Cookies

This website loads Google Fonts, which may set cookies according to Google's own privacy and cookie policies. No other third-party cookies are used unless you have accepted non-essential cookies.

3. Cookie Consent

When you first visit this website, a cookie consent banner is displayed. You can choose to:

  • Accept All Cookies — enables both essential and analytics cookies
  • Decline — only essential cookies will be used

No non-essential tracking scripts are loaded until you make an active choice to accept.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy → Cookies

To reset your cookie consent on this website, clear your browser's localStorage for this site, and the consent banner will appear again on your next visit.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised accordingly.
